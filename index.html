Waterborne biohazard signal → CDS Hooks card, in real time

A real-time river/catchment contamination signal drives a FHIR R4 RiskAssessment, downstream propagation forecasting via a 1D advection–dispersion transport model (independently validated against a direct numerical PDE solution), and a live CDS Hooks patient-view card in the EHR — before a clinician would otherwise have any reason to suspect waterborne exposure at a patient's home address.

Why this case study, why now: the real European problem

  1. Flooding is a growing, quantified public-health risk across the EU, and it specifically elevates waterborne-pathogen exposure. Heavy rainfall events make it twice as likely to have harmful pathogen concentrations in EU water bodies, from contaminated run-off and combined sewage overflows. European Environment Agency, eea.europa.eu (fetched directly)
  2. The Mondego at Coimbra has real, measured, published contamination — pharmaceutical concentrations rose ~40× immediately downstream of Coimbra's wastewater treatment plant, with one compound reaching a "high risk to aquatic life" score in that stretch. Kötke et al. 2024, Heliyon 10(15):e34825, DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e34825
  3. Coimbra coordinates a real, active EUR 4.9M Horizon Europe research programme (OneAquaHealth, CORDIS grant 101086521, 2023–2026) built on exactly this premise: urban freshwater ecosystem health and human One Health outcomes are linked. This prototype is not a deliverable of that grant, but targets the same city and reuses the FHIR IG its standards partner, HL7 Europe, publishes — disclosed honestly against the real project's own published scope. cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101086521 (fetched directly); see METHODS.md §6b
